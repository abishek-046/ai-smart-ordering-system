## Cross-Session Synthesis

### Recurring patterns observed
1. **Demand concentration:** Both peak sessions showed 80% of customers in a 15–20 min window. Off-peak is empty.
2. **Information vacuum:** Students arrive without knowing queue, availability, or wait time. Staff operate without knowing demand volume.
3. **Compound delays:** One error (wrong item, sold-out discovery, new preparation) creates a ripple that delays every subsequent customer.
4. **Academic impact:** In 2 of 3 peak sessions, at least one student was directly observed missing or arriving late to class due to canteen delay.
5. **Self-adaptation tax:** Students who have learned to avoid peak hours (Session 3 pair) pay a cost — they come during class break time, eating into study/rest time.
